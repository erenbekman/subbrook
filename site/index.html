O C Y N E S i G F

Know what you pay, before you pay it.

The month at a glance

One total for the month, what's already paid and what's still to come — with your share of shared plans, not the full price.

A calendar of charges

Every renewal on the day it lands, with week totals. Mark a charge as paid, skipped or refunded.

Reminders that arrive on time

A heads-up before each renewal and before a free trial turns into a charge. Subbrook waits quietly in the menu bar.

Import a bank statement

Drop in a CSV, Excel or PDF export and Subbrook finds the charges that repeat. The file is read on your computer and never uploaded.

Every currency, one total

Pay in lira, dollars, euros or pounds — totals convert to your currency with central bank rates (TCMB, ECB).

Price rises, noticed

Every price change is kept, so a plan that quietly got more expensive doesn't stay quiet.

Take it anywhere

Export to CSV, add renewals to your calendar with an .ics file, or share a poster of your month.

Keyboard first

⌘K finds any subscription or command. ⌘N adds one. ⌘1–4 moves between views.

Privacy

Your subscriptions are nobody else's business.

There is no Subbrook account and no Subbrook server. Everything lives in a single SQLite file in your app-data folder — back it up, sync it, or delete it, it's yours.

  • No account. Open the app and start adding.
  • No telemetry. Nothing about you or your use is collected.
  • One network request. Daily exchange rates from TCMB and ECB, and only when you track more than one currency.
  • Open source. MIT licensed — read every line on GitHub.

Free. Not free for three.

No paywall, no premium tier, no limit on how many subscriptions you track. Subbrook is built in the open and stays that way.
